Rice University has received a **$4.5 million NSF award** to develop
**[Houdini](https://houdini-sdr.org)**, an open-access software-defined
radio platform supporting multiple spectrum bands simultaneously. The
modular system will let researchers across wireless networking,
sensing, and imaging prototype 6G technologies without building
expensive custom equipment for each experiment. The project is led by
**Ashutosh Sabharwal** with co-PIs **Joseph Cavallaro** and **Rahman
Doost-Mohammady**.

[Read the full announcement on Rice News](https://news.rice.edu/news/2024/legos-wireless-research-rice-led-university-consortium-tackles-future-wireless-45m-nsf).
